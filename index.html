What you need to know

Where to book

Online only, at singaporeair.com or the SingaporeAir app. Not through KrisFlyer Membership Services, ticket offices or travel agents. Book and ticket in the same session.

No changes, no refunds

Changes aren't allowed, and neither are cancellations, even for partly or fully unused tickets. Make sure of your dates first.

Whole itinerary must qualify

If any leg is outside 1–30 Nov, is ticketed after 31 Oct, or is on a flight that isn't listed, the whole booking loses the discount. Book legs separately if needed.

Flight numbers must match

The discount only applies to the flight numbers listed. On a return booking, both sectors must be listed flights. Codeshares don't qualify.

Miles only, plus taxes

Tickets must be paid fully in miles (no miles + cash). Taxes and fees are charged on top and aren't discounted.

Other limits

One-way or return, no stopovers, no waitlisting, can't be combined with other promotions. Seats are limited and go to whoever books first.

How to redeem

  1. Log in to KrisFlyer on singaporeair.com or the app.
  2. Select Book a Flight → Redeem flights and enter your trip.
  3. Pick flights in the Promo fare column. The discount shows in the booking summary.
  4. Check out to confirm. Ticketing has to happen immediately.
